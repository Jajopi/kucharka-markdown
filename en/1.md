# Some beginning words

Welcome to the Dormitory Guidebook, which provides a lot of useful information relating to life at the dormitory.

This guidebook was prepared for you by the Dormitory Council. It was usually written in Czech, but in 2022 it was translated into English (thanks to CU Point). Since then, it was updated by the Dormitory Council several times. However, it is possible that you may find some errors – for example, some links might have a better alternative in English, some information may be a little dated or not accurate for international students. If you come across any deficiencies or have any suggestions for improving this guidebook, we would be grateful if you could reach out to us at the following e-mail address: <kr@listopad.koleje.cuni.cz> so we can fix it.

Do you want to stay informed about what’s happening at the dormitory, but don’t want to constantly check Facebook (whose algorithm, moreover, shows only some posts)? Sign up for the *Dormitory Council’s email newsletters*! How

- [kr-oznam@listopad.koleje.cuni.cz](https://gimli.ms.mff.cuni.cz/services/mailman3/postorius/lists/kr-oznam.listopad.koleje.cuni.cz/) is newsletter with important updates from the dormitory management and the Dormitory council;

- [kr-akce@listopad.koleje.cuni.cz](https://gimli.ms.mff.cuni.cz/services/mailman3/postorius/lists/kr-akce.listopad.koleje.cuni.cz/) is newsletter notifying about featured events within the dormitory and its surroundings.
